LUCKY STARS CHAMPIONS
Fusion Keywords

These effects apply to the Main and Concentrated fusion chains at L1–L10.

MAIN FUSIONS

Sky and Field keywords set the environment on face-up entry or Flip.
Aura, Lock and Negate apply while the source is face-up and active.

SUN
Sun Sky (Dragon) — Set the Sky to Sun.
Mountain (Thunder) — Set the Field to Mountain.
Moon Aura (Warrior) — Opposing creatures are treated as Moon.
Mercury Lock (Rock) — Opposing Mercury creatures cannot attack.
Mercury Negate (Pyro) — Negate opposing Mercury creature effects.

MOON
Moon Sky (Fiend) — Set the Sky to Moon.
Umi (Zombie) — Set the Field to Umi.
Venus Aura (Sea Serpent) — Opposing creatures are treated as Venus.
Sun Lock (Fish) — Opposing Sun creatures cannot attack.
Sun Negate (Aqua) — Negate opposing Sun creature effects.

VENUS
Venus Sky (Beast-Warrior) — Set the Sky to Venus.
Forest (Winged Beast) — Set the Field to Forest.
Mercury Aura (Beast) — Opposing creatures are treated as Mercury.
Moon Lock (Plant) — Opposing Moon creatures cannot attack.
Moon Negate (Fairy) — Negate opposing Moon creature effects.

MERCURY
Mercury Sky (Machine) — Set the Sky to Mercury.
Wasteland (Dinosaur) — Set the Field to Wasteland.
Sun Aura (Spellcaster) — Opposing creatures are treated as Sun.
Venus Lock (Insect) — Opposing Venus creatures cannot attack.
Venus Negate (Reptile) — Negate opposing Venus creature effects.

CONCENTRATED FUSIONS

Each player gets their own attempt at a paired effect. Low Direct is an
ongoing rule while its source is active.

SUN
Top Summon (Dragon) — Each player summons their strongest creature from their
Deck with its effects active.
Creature Return (Thunder) — Return your weakest creature and the opponent’s strongest creature to their owners’ hands, using their current battle position.
Up H-Cascade (Warrior) — Each player summons one creature from hand at the
nearest available LVL above this creature's LVL.
Each top ATK: -250/+250. (Rock, Guard Shift) — Each player's highest-ATK
creature permanently loses 250 ATK and gains 250 DEF.
Burn (Pyro) — Each player takes damage equal to half this creature's current ATK.

MOON
Front Morph (Fiend) — Destroy a creature on each side, then attempt an
Equal D-Cascade for that side at the destroyed creature's LVL.
Wight (Zombie) — Each player receives one Wight Token.
Low Direct (Sea Serpent) — Either player can attack directly with a creature
whose LVL is below this creature's LVL.
Equal H-Cascade (Fish) — Each player summons one creature from hand at this
creature's LVL.
Duplicate (Aqua) — Each player makes a copy of their strongest creature.
The copy has its effects negated and cannot trigger Duplicate.

VENUS
Equip (Beast-Warrior) — Each player sets the first Equip from their Deck.
Down H-Cascade (Winged Beast) — Each player summons one creature from hand
at the nearest available LVL below this creature's LVL.
Evolve (Beast) — Each player advances one eligible creature by one LVL in
its Type. A Concentrated creature advances along the Concentrated chain.
Other creatures advance along the Main chain. The result's effects are negated.
Plant Token (Plant) — Each player receives one Plant Token.
Half Heal (Fairy) — Each player gains LP equal to half this creature's current ATK.

MERCURY
Trap Morph (Machine) — Each player destroys a back-row card, then attempts
to set a Trap from their Deck.
Egg (Dinosaur) — Each player receives one Egg Token.
Each Sets 1 Spell from Deck. (Spellcaster) — Each player sets the first
Spell from their Deck in their own back row.
Flip (Insect) — On summon or face-up Flip, flip every other creature
in both front rows at once.
Petrify (Reptile) — Petrify each player's strongest creature. It cannot
attack or change its own battle position while Petrified. An external Flip
or leaving the field ends Petrify.

H-Cascade summons from hand. D-Cascade summons from Deck.
Up and Down use the nearest eligible LVL in that direction. Equal uses the
specified LVL exactly. Cascade recruits keep their effects.

Tokens created on the effect controller's side enter face-up in Defense.
Tokens given to their opponent enter face-up in Attack.

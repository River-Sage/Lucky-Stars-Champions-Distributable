Cascade & Tokens

Cascade

H-Cascade summons from hand. D-Cascade summons from Deck. Up and Down use the nearest eligible LVL in that direction. Equal uses the specified LVL exactly. Cascade recruits keep their effects.

Token positions

Tokens created on the effect controller's side enter face-up in Defense. Tokens given to their opponent enter face-up in Attack.
